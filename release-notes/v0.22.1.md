# v0.22.1 — unit cards in the sheet, small fixes

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.22.1** — AI coach for Age of Empires II: DE.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.1-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat` (replaces the previous version; settings and key stay).
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.1-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.1-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **Unit cards in the match sheet**: when a message names units, the sheet adds a *Units* block straight from the current-patch data — HP, attack (and range), armour, speed, cost, age, train time, bonus damage (biggest first) and whether your civ or the rival's can make it. Questions like "cuánta vida tiene el piquero" or "what does the mangudai cost" open with that block and read its first line aloud.
- The map block shows the in-game minimap picture (same pictures as the guide).
- Answer costs under a cent are shown with four decimals instead of "$0.000".

See v0.22.0 for the match sheet and the screen reading (OCR).
