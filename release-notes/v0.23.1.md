# v0.23.1 — local screen reading tuned on the real game HUD

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.23.1** — coach for Age of Empires II: DE, with or without AI.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.1-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat` (replaces the previous version; settings and key stay).
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.1-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.1-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **Local OCR now reads the real Definitive Edition bar.** v0.23.0 was tuned on synthetic captures whose layout did not match the game: in DE the villager counts are **yellow** and the same size as the stockpiles (one under each resource icon), the total villager count is **cyan** under the population icon, the idle-villager **bell** comes after the population, the age name is yellow, and the **game clock sits at the top centre of the screen, outside the bar** (`00:12:30`, with the speed and difficulty in parentheses in single player). The reader now separates text by colour, maps the fields by that layout, reads the clock with hours, and *Calibrate* finds the band of resource boxes under the wooden frame and always extends the region to the clock. Verified on a 2560×1600 capture and its 1080p/768p downscales: every field correct (stockpiles, villagers per resource, total villagers, population, idle, age, clock), ~0.4 s per reading. The AI engine's prompt describes the same layout; both engines report the total villager count (shown next to the population in the HUD line and given to the coach).
- *Mark by hand* now tells you to reach past the clock at the top centre.
- Guide → Meta by civ: build cards show the step count and the Feudal population ("9 steps · Feudal at 17 pop") instead of a bare "17 pop".
- Tests: real-HUD fixtures replace the synthetic ones; the settings migration to the answer modes (a key from v0.22 keeps the AI sheet) and the guide's "Load in the panel" button are covered.

Still untested: other HUD scales, other game languages (the age name), late-game numbers (4–5 digits, "150/200"), UI mods — send a screenshot if it misreads yours.

See v0.23.0 for the answer modes, the local OCR, the build-order card and the guide changes.
