# v0.22.0 — the match sheet (structured answers from the data) and screen reading (OCR)

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.22.0** — AI coach for Age of Empires II: DE.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.0-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat` (replaces the previous version; settings and key stay).
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.0-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.22.0-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **Match sheet — new default answer mode.** Every message now gets a sheet built only from the app's data, nothing written by a model: counters to the units you name (or to the rival's registered army, or to the rival civ's typical units), your civ (strength by age, bonuses, unique unit and techs, what it lacks, the profile's opening for this kind of map), the rival civ (the same plus "how to play against it"), the map (what wins there, standard openings, timings, the line about your rival's civ) and suggested builds: 3 meta step-by-step builds that fit your civ, the map type and the profile's opening — one click loads them into the build panel — plus anti-meta guides for your civ. With an API key the AI only parses your message (Haiku, a fraction of a cent); without a key a keyword parser does it, so the sheet works on the web with no key at all. The 3 most useful lines are read aloud.
- **"Ask the AI for advice"** (button under every sheet, or `/advice`): the free coach answers as before, with the sheet in its prompt so the advice cites it (the bonus, the missing tech, the map property, the counter) instead of starting over.
- **Settings → Brain → Chat answers: Sheet (data) / Free (AI)**, also `/mode sheet|free`. Free mode is the v0.21 behaviour, unchanged. `/sheet` shows the sheet for the current match; it is also posted when a match is detected.
- **Screen reading (OCR, desktop, experimental, off by default)** — Settings → Advanced → *Screen reading*: every 10–60 s the app captures the game's resource bar, sends only that crop to your AI provider's vision model (Claude Haiku, ~US$0.0003 per reading) and shows wood/food/gold/stone, villagers on each, population, idle villagers, age and game clock under the state line. The loaded build's step compares its villager allocation with the bar ("+2 🪵 for the step"), the build clock syncs itself to the game clock, the registered age moves forward when the bar shows a new one, and the coach gets the fresh numbers in its prompt. *Calibrate* finds the bar on your screen once (manual default region otherwise), *Read now* shows what it reads; readings are skipped while the picture does not change and the loop backs off when the game is not on screen. Needs the game in borderless window and the app visible; on Mac, screen-recording permission.
- Commands in the help: `/sheet` (`/ficha`), `/advice` (`/consejo`), `/mode` (`/modo`).
