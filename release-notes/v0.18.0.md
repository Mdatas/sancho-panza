# v0.18.0 — clearer at a glance, map pictures

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.18.0** — AI coach for Age of Empires II: DE.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.18.0-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat`.
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.18.0-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.18.0-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **4 v 4 bar you can read**: captions "Your team" (blue) and "Enemies" (red), your own shield rimmed in gold, every slot labelled with its civ (or You / Ally / Enemy), a one-line hint until you fill it in, and a VS button that opens the counters.
- **Counters panel** in two columns, "They make → You answer with", red chips vs blue chips, collapsible; shown only when there is something to compute.
- **Tabs with icons** (Chat, Build orders, Settings, Guide) that no longer wrap or shrink.
- **Build-order cards** tell step-by-step builds (≡) from video guides (▶) apart, with a colour bar for META / anti-meta / MEME.
- After the API-key window closes, the app lands in the **Guide**.
- **Guide**: the real in-game minimap for 120+ of the 129 maps (Liquipedia / Fandom wikis, © Microsoft; the few without one keep an emblem for their type), building icons in the lineups, a phone layout for the tabs.
- Web version: the Guide is a tab inside the app (no separate window) and new versions load on the next refresh.
