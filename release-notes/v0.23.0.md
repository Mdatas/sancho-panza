# v0.23.0 — local mode (no AI needed), local screen reading, build orders made for mid-game, Meta by civ

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.23.0** — coach for Age of Empires II: DE, with or without AI.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.0-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat` (replaces the previous version; settings and key stay).
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.0-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.23.0-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **Three answer modes, and the default needs no AI.** The welcome popup (and Settings → Brain, or `/mode`) now offers **Local** (new default): the match sheet built from the data, your message read by a keyword parser — civs, maps and units in Spanish and English, "I'm X vs Y", allies and enemies, age, ELO — no key, no cost, nothing leaves your PC; **Sheet + AI**: the same sheet, but Claude Haiku parses the message (free text, typos, mixed languages; ~US$0.0002 per message) and "Ask the AI for advice" is one click away; **Free coach (AI)**: the free answer of v0.21 (~US$0.03–0.05 with Sonnet). Each mode explains what it does, what it sends and what it costs before you pick it; the API key is only asked for when you switch to an AI mode. Players who already had a key configured keep the AI sheet. `/mode local|sheet|free` (`/modo local|ficha|libre`); `/mode` alone cycles.
- **Local screen reading (OCR).** The resource-bar reader now has two engines (Settings → Advanced): **Local** (default) reads the digits on your own PC with Tesseract — free, ~0.3 s per reading, the crop never leaves the machine; the app isolates each number itself (adaptive threshold, connected components, per-word recognition with a digit whitelist), since the bar's small white digits defeat whole-line OCR. **AI** (the vision model of your provider, v0.22 behaviour) stays as an option. *Calibrate* finds the bar locally by its dark strip; if it misses, **Mark by hand** lets you click the two corners of the bar on a screenshot; *Test* shows the crop with a box around every word it read. The local engine was tuned on synthetic HUD captures — if it misreads your screen, send a screenshot. The download grows ~7 MB (Tesseract and its English model, both Apache-2.0).
- **Build orders, easier to read mid-game.** New **step card** in the overlay: ‹ › arrows, step number, age and the step's clock, villagers as icons (👤 🍖 🪵 🪙 🪨), the step text, the **next step** in small print, a countdown to it when the build clock runs, and a ▾ button that folds the card to one line. The full panel lists **all the steps** (click one to jump). Finding one: the catalogue is sorted by relevance to your match (your civ's builds first, then meta), and with a civ and a map set a **"For your match"** group at the top shows the builds that fit your civ, the map type and the profile's opening — the same ones the sheet suggests.
- **Guide: "Pro tips" and "Meta by civ".** *Topics* is now **Pro tips** (the 24 topic summaries). *Meta* is now **Meta by civ**: pick a civ and see its meta opening for open, closed and water maps with the step-by-step builds that follow it (**Load in the panel** sends one straight to the build panel), its Castle and Imperial plan, its compositions, how it is played against, its patch win rate and its guides by tier (meta, anti-meta, memes); the full win-rate/pick-rate table of the patch is folded underneath.
- Sheet parser improvements from 0.22.1 (team roles "somos X y Y vs Z", "X vs Y" → X is you, stats questions never register an enemy army) ship in the desktop app with this version.

See v0.22.0 for the match sheet and v0.22.1 for the unit cards.
