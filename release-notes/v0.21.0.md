# v0.21.0 — the tavern look: four appearances, switchable in Settings

**Sancho Panza · Age of Empires 2 Fan Companion v0.21.0** — AI coach for Age of Empires II: DE.

### Downloads
- **Windows**: `SanchoPanza-0.21.0-windows-x64.zip` → unzip → double-click `Instalar.bat`.
- **Mac with Apple chip (M1–M4)**: `SanchoPanza-0.21.0-mac-arm64.zip` · **Mac with Intel**: `SanchoPanza-0.21.0-mac-x64.zip` → drag to Applications → first launch: System Settings → Privacy & Security → "Open Anyway".
- **Android / iPhone / any browser**: https://mdatas.github.io/sancho-panza/ → "Add to Home screen".

### Changes
- **Tavern** (new default look): a daylight market street — oak signs hanging from an iron rail for the tabs (the active one hangs lower), a striped awning with an oak beam for the title bar, the 4 v 4 bar on a dark plank with heraldic shields and a brass VS medallion, the conversation on a parchment sheet (your notes on kraft, the coach's answers sealed in red), brass buttons, build steps on a ledger page with the villager tally on a plank.
- **Tavern at night**: the same street after dark — stone and lamplight, crimson buttons, lit signs.
- **Classic dark** (the previous look) and **Classic light** (a daylight version of it) stay available.
- Switch in **Settings → Appearance**; the guide window and the web version follow. Everything is drawn with CSS (no game art), with the Almendra typeface (SIL OFL) for the signs.
- Guide: the section tabs are never squeezed into initials any more — they move under the search box when the window is narrow.
