# Economy benchmarks (1v1, ~1000-1600 ELO targets)

Villager counts: ~20 at Feudal click (scouts) / 22 (archers) / 27 (FC); ~30 arriving in Castle; 60-80 by early Imperial; 100-120 late game on big maps. Never stop villager production before ~100-120 pop unless you are all-in.

Gather rates per villager per minute (field measurements, Guía del Age / community): wood 23.4 base → 28.1 Double-Bit → 33.7 Bow Saw → 37.1 Two-Man Saw; farms 20.4 base → 22.7 Wheelbarrow → 24.0 Hand Cart; gold 22.8 → 26.2 Gold Mining → 30.2 Shaft Mining; stone 21.6 → 24.8 → 28.6; hunting 24.6; sheep 19.8; berries 18.6; shore fish 25.8; fishing ship (deep fish) 29.4. Real numbers drop 5-15% with long walks and bumping.

Farm food: 175 base → 250 Horse Collar → 375 Heavy Plow → 550 Crop Rotation (plus the villager carry bonus). Horse Collar before your first ~6 farms; Heavy Plow early Castle when you have 15+ farms.

Resource splits by composition (rough, use eco_plan for exact):
- Scouts/Knights: ~55% food, 20% wood, 25% gold. 2 stables of knights need ≈ 12-13 farmers + 6-7 on gold (Castle age rates).
- Archers/Crossbows: ~40% wood, 10% food (vills + farms), 50% gold... in practice 10-12 on wood, 8-10 on gold for 2-3 ranges, rest on food for villagers/upgrades.
- Men-at-arms/militia: food heavy with a little gold (60f 20g).
- Trash (halbs/skirms): food+wood only — move gold miners to farms when gold runs out.
- Siege: wood + gold (mangonel 160w 135g); a siege workshop running needs ~8 on wood.
- Monks: 100 gold each — 4-5 gold miners per monastery in use.

Transitions (what to move): archers → knights: add 2 stables, shift 6-8 from wood to farms, keep gold. Knights → halbs/skirms in late Imperial: move gold miners to farms and wood. Feudal → Castle: you need 800 food 200 gold banked; 3 on gold is enough early, start a mining camp 2-3 minutes before clicking.

Market: use it to fix a single-resource shortage, not as a strategy; selling stone early (if not trushing) is standard; buying food to click up is fine when the opening is late.

Idle time: check the TC every 25 seconds (one villager); each idle minute at 2 TCs costs you ~3 villagers of eco for the rest of the game.
