# Current-meta tips: patch 185872 (The Viking Sagas, 2026-09-22)

Fetched 2026-10-16. Liquipedia and reddit were not used (blocked). **No named pro or caster post-patch strategy commentary was reachable in text form.** Viper's reaction video is pre-gameplay first impressions only. Treat any "pro says" claim as UNVERIFIED.

## 1. What changed this patch (official, for coaching context)
- Source: https://www.ageofempires.com/news/age-of-empires-ii-definitive-edition-update-185872/

### New civs and units
- **New civs:**
  - Danes: infantry and siege. Unique unit is the Jomsviking.
  - Saxons: infantry and defenses. Unique unit is the Hearth Troop.
  - Varangians: cavalry and navy. Unique unit is the Jarl, an axe-throwing cavalry unit strong vs infantry.
  - Unique-unit descriptions come from https://www.ageofempires.com/news/the-warriors-of-the-viking-sagas/
  - Detailed civ bonus numbers were NOT on the official pages fetched. The Fandom wiki fetch failed, so these are UNREACHABLE.
- **Mounted Crossbowman (new regional unit):**
  - Replaces Cavalry Archers for most European civs: Britons, Burgundians, Celts, Franks, Italians, Poles, Portuguese, Sicilians, Spanish, Teutons, Vikings and Bohemians.
  - Described as having a "powerful but slow attack".
  - New tech **Cranequins** gives +1 range and +2 attack vs infantry. It goes to Britons, Franks and Sicilians.
- **Varangian Guard:** shock infantry that generates gold while fighting. Available to Vikings, Byzantines and all three DLC civs.

### General balance changes
- Anti-shock-infantry bonuses were raised:
  - Man-at-Arms +3
  - Long Swordsman +8
  - Two-Handed Swordsman +9
  - Champion +9
- Eagle Scout train time: 50s to 46s.
- Onager: attack 55, pierce armor 8.
- Ram line of sight: 3 to 5.

### Notable civ changes
- **Franks:**
  - Throwing Axeman range: 3 to 5 (Elite 4 to 6).
  - Bearded Axe removed.
  - New tech Ordonnance Companies: Mounted Crossbowmen cost 40% less gold.
- **Teutons:** the armor bonus now applies to infantry and mounted units. Elite Teutonic Knight melee armor: 10 to 8.
- **Magyars:** Huszar train time 12s to 18s.
- **Vietnamese:** the +20% HP bonus now applies only to foot archers and skirmishers. Rattan Archers lose HP.
- **Byzantines:** Cataphract bonus vs infantry raised to 13 (Elite 18).
- **Vikings:**
  - Lose Gambesons, Cavalry Archers and Cannon Galleons.
  - Chieftains no longer generates gold.
- **Bohemians:** Hussite Wagon and Wagenburg Tactics nerfed.
- **Spanish:** Conquistadors are slower to train and cost more.
- **Portuguese:** lose Redemption. Organ Gun train time goes to 28s.

## 2. Meta snapshot from data (ageofnotes, patch 185872, ~4-16 Oct 2026)
Details are in insights_civ_stats.md.

- **Strongest overall:** Romans 54.6%, Armenians 53.5%, Bulgarians 53.3%, Malians 53.2%, Turks 53.1%. Source: https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ-win-rates/
- **Strong at 1800+ Elo:**
  - Mongols 54.3% (683 games)
  - Romans 55.3% (190)
  - Turks 53.5% (271)
  - Armenians drop to 45.1% (257)
  - INFERENCE: Armenians are a low-Elo stomp civ.
- **Weakest overall:** Muisca, Britons, Gurjaras, Saracens, Incas, Vietnamese, Byzantines and Varangians, all ≤46.9%.
- **Under-performing changed civs:**
  - Byzantines at 46.7%. The Cataphract buff did not lift them.
  - Vietnamese at 46.7%, and 40.7% on Arena.
  - Britons at 46.5%, and 42.8% on Arena.
  - Varangians at 46.9%, and 37.3% at 1800+.
- **Game-length profiles:**
  - Romans are strongest early: <20 min 58.7%, 20-30 min 57.3%, dropping to 50.1% at 40-50 min. Source: https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/romans/
  - Danes spike at 20-30 min (56.0%), then fall to 43.5% at 40-50 min. Source: https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/danes/
  - Saxons are weak under 20 min (47.9%) and better at 20-30 min (52.6%) and 60-75 min (51.8%). Source: https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/saxons/
  - Varangians are weak in the midgame (30-40 min 43.9%) and recover late (50-60 min 51.7%).

## 5. Counter guidance (data-backed; matchups from ageofnotes patch 185872)
- **vs Romans** (worst matchups for Romans, from https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/romans/):
  - Turks 43.3% (90 games)
  - Celts 44.4% (63)
  - Mongols 46.0% (224)
  - Pick Advisor says "no hard counter".
  - Romans' win rate falls at 40-50 min, so the advice is to survive the early aggression and take the game long. INFERENCE from the game-length data.
  - What Romans build: Militia-line plus Spearman plus Scorpion (aoe2insights).
  - Generic counter logic is UNVERIFIED for this patch: archers or skirmishers plus Mangonels vs the infantry and Scorpion mass, and Hand Cannons or Cataphract-type units late.
- **vs Mongols** (worst matchups for Mongols, from https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/mongols/):
  - Dravidians 41.6% (101)
  - Italians 42.0% (219)
  - Poles 42.9% (191)
  - Mayans 44.3% (230)
  - The Pick Advisor's top pick vs Mongols is Hindustanis.
  - What Mongols build: Scout, Mangudai/Cavalry Archer, Steppe Lancer (aoe2insights).
- **vs Armenians** (worst matchups, from https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/armenians/):
  - Dravidians 41.0% (39)
  - Khmer 43.9% (123)
  - Mayans 46.3% (67)
  - The Pick Advisor's pick is Turks.
- **vs Turks** (worst matchups, from https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/turks/):
  - Gurjaras 36.0% (50)
  - Wei 41.0% (39)
  - Burmese 42.9% (42)
  - Cumans 44.2% (113)
  - Turks are weakest on open maps: Arabian Desert 48.2%, Arabia 49.7%.
- **vs Bulgarians:**
  - The Pick Advisor's pick is Romans (51.3%).
  - Franks lose to Bulgarians badly, at 35.1% over 202 games. Source: https://ageofnotes.com/stats/civ/franks/
- **vs Saxons:** Jurchens 34.3% (35), Portuguese 41.3% (80), Armenians 41.4% (87).
- **vs Danes:** Turks 32.4% (105), Armenians 34.7% (95), Japanese 38.2% (131).
- **vs Varangians:** Chinese 35.3% (116), Ethiopians 36.2% (163), Malians 37.3%, Khitans 37.3%.
  - Mongols beat Varangians 58.5% (448 games).
- **Patch mechanics with counter implications:** Man-at-Arms, Long Swordsmen and Champions all gained bonus vs shock infantry (see section 1). The obvious target is the Varangian Guard, which Saxons, Danes, Vikings and Byzantines field. The patch notes do not spell this out, so INFERENCE.
