# Current 1v1 build orders: villager allocations and timings

Patch context: Update 185872 (22 Sep 2026). It added Mounted Crossbowmen, the shock-infantry bonus buffs, Eagle Scout 46s, and the Huszar, Flemish Militia and Chieftains nerfs. **None of the build-order sources below carries a 2026 patch tag.** The standard Dark Age openings were not touched by the patch notes (I checked the general and economy sections of https://www.ageofempires.com/news/age-of-empires-ii-definitive-edition-update-185872/: no villager, gather-rate or age-up cost changes). Applying them to the current patch is therefore an inference, and is **UNVERIFIED**.

Sources:
- **[CIC]** Cicero's Build Order Reference, mirrored by "Joh". Each page carries graded A+..E benchmark times, but no publication date. https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/
- **[AOC]** aoecompanion.com build guides. Steps by villager count, no clock times, no date. https://aoecompanion.com/build-guides
- **[SUB]** Substack scout guide (BJL, 2024-05-04). https://ageofempires.substack.com/p/aoe2-scout-rush-guide
- **[STM]** Steam guides: ptee 2023-06 https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2993319677 ; Akfiz 2023-08 https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3017973327

Pop notation: "22 pop" = 21 villagers + scout, clicking Feudal after Loom ([STM] ptee). "27+2" = 27 pop at the Feudal click, plus 2 villagers made during Feudal before the Castle click ([CIC] usage).

## 1. Scouts: 21 pop [CIC] https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/21-pop-scouts.html
| Step | W | F | G |
|---|---|---|---|
| 6 sheep | | 6 | |
| 3 wood | 3 | 6 | |

| 4 berries | 3 | 12 | |

| Feudal: stable, scouts; Double-Bit Axe + Horse Collar; farms to 14 | 10 | 18 | |
| 5 to gold, 2 berry villagers → farms; Wheelbarrow, blacksmith | 10 | 18 | 5 |

**Benchmarks (A+ / A / B / C / D / E):**
- 21-pop Feudal click: 7:35 / 7:45 / 7:55 / 8:10 / 8:35 / 9:05
- 6 scouts: 13:05 / 13:20 / 13:35 / 13:55 / 14:25 / 15:05
- Castle click: 16:30 / 16:55 / 17:20 / 17:50 / 18:20 / 19:00

Notes [CIC]: in 1v1 you may need to switch to ranged units, or go straight up to Castle after scouts. Deer or eco-bonus civs allow faster builds.

Variant [SUB], 18 pop (credited to Morley Games): click Feudal "as soon as you hit 500 food". On the way up, move 5 sheep villagers to wood, 2 to straggler trees, and 1 to build barracks + house. In Feudal, make scouts nonstop. "Four is an optimal number." Then aim for 15 farms and Castle.

Variant [AOC], Feudal at 21 villagers: https://aoecompanion.com/build-guides/scout-rush

## 4. Drush → Archers: 28 pop [CIC] https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/28-pop-drush-archers.html
- **Dark Age:** 6 sheep / 4 wood / 1 lures boar / 4 berries. 1 villager mines 10 gold, then goes to food. 1 builds barracks, train 3 militia. +1 wood (5W). Rest on sheep, 3 to farms (5W / 19F). Loom → Feudal.
- **Advancing:** wood to 11 (2 lumber camps), 4 to gold, 2 on stragglers.
- **Feudal:** 2 ranges, archers, Double-Bit Axe, gold to 8, blacksmith + Fletching, farms, Wheelbarrow (14W / 16F / 8G).

**Benchmarks:**
- 28-pop Feudal (including 3 militia): 9:15 / 9:25 / 9:35 / 9:50 / 10:15 / 10:45
- 6 archers + Fletching: 14:20 / 14:40 / 15:05 / 15:30 / 16:00 / 16:35
- Castle click with 22 archers: 19:15 / 19:45 / 20:15 / 20:45 / 21:25 / 22:15

Goal [CIC]: delay the opponent. Villager kills are a bonus.

## 5. Drush → Fast Castle: 32+2 [CIC] https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/32+2-drush-fc.html
- **Dark Age:** 6 sheep / 4 wood / 1 lures boar / 4 berries. 1 mines 10 gold. 1 builds barracks then walls, train 3 militia. +1 to walling. Wood to 8. Rest on sheep/stragglers, farms to 8. Loom → Feudal.
- **Advancing:** 4 to gold, +2 wood, +2 berries (10W / 14F / 4G).
- **Feudal:** range + blacksmith, +2 gold. Click Castle. Gold to 9, archers, Double-Bit Axe, 2nd range, Fletching.
- **Castle:** Crossbow + Bodkin.

**Benchmarks:**
- 3 militia: 8:40 / 8:50 / 9:00 / 9:15 / 9:40 / 10:10
- 32-pop Feudal with walls: 10:55 / 11:05 / 11:15 / 11:30 / 11:55 / 12:25
- 32+2 Castle: 14:00 / 14:10 / 14:25 / 14:40 / 15:10 / 15:45
- 8 crossbowmen + Bodkin: 18:00 / 18:20 / 18:45 / 19:10 / 19:40 / 20:15

Notes [CIC]: walling is required. Eco is stretched at Castle, so delay Bow Saw or Bodkin.

[AOC] variant: 3 militia around villager 17, Feudal at 28, 8 gold / 12 wood on the way to Castle. https://aoecompanion.com/build-guides/drush-fast-castle

## 8. Towers: 19 pop [CIC] https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/19-pop-towers.html
6 sheep / 2 wood / 1 lures boar (+2 sheep/boar) / 2 berries / rest on sheep/boar. Loom → Feudal. On the way up: 2 to stone, +2 wood, send 6 villagers forward. In Feudal: stone to 5. The page is truncated.

[CIC] calls it "A pretty all-in strategy", "not particularly recommended now that towers have reduced HP in Feudal". No benchmarks.

## 10. Timing sanity notes
- [STM] ptee: at 22 pop (21 villagers + scout + Loom) the minimum Feudal time is "10:05". Each villager or Loom takes 25 s. Cicero's 8:00 figure for the same pop is the Feudal *click* benchmark. The two sources likely measure different moments (click vs arrival). **UNVERIFIED**: ptee does not say which.
- [STM] Akfiz: Feudal at about 20–21 villagers, Castle at about 33–37 villagers, Imperial at about 70 villagers or 30 farms, villagers to about 130 late game.
