# AoE2 DE map pool and per-map civ win rates, patch 185872

All data was fetched on 2026-10-16. The source is ageofnotes.com, which uses unofficial ranked ladder data and excludes mirror matches.

**Data window:** patch 185872 (released 2026-09-22). Several pages say recording began **2026-10-04**, so the window is about 4 Oct to 16 Oct 2026. Cells marked "few" have fewer than 500 games.

## 3. Meta civs per map (INFERENCE from the tables above: high WR plus meaningful sample)
- **Arabia:** Romans, Bulgarians, Goths, Mayans, Hindustanis, Chinese. Avoid Bohemians.
- **Arabian Desert:** Bulgarians, Vikings, Armenians, Huns, Mongols (the most picked). Avoid Britons, Poles and Tatars.
- **Glade:** Armenians, Malians, Romans, Turks, Teutons, Bulgarians. Avoid Byzantines and Incas.
- **Arena:** Turks, Cumans, Teutons, Khmer, Romans, Malay. Avoid Vietnamese, Britons and Varangians.
- **Fortress:** Turks, Spanish, Japanese, Khmer, Saxons.
- **Scandinavia:** Hindustanis, Romans, Japanese, Celts, Malians.
- **Team RM:** Cumans, Dravidians, Armenians, Bulgarians, Mongols, Huns, Magyars (see insights_civ_stats.md section 6).
