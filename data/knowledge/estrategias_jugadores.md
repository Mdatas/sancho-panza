## (b) Responding to each opening without overplaying

### Scouts
- **Walls are the best defence.** Use resource palisades around the lumber camp and mill, or a full wall. Make 3–4 spears, but don't overinvest, because the opponent can switch to archers. Source: klnek, 2020-08-30, https://steamcommunity.com/app/813780/discussions/0/2914346777818133576
- **2–3 spearmen is enough.** Source: Nico, same thread.
- **Wall earlier, from about 15 pop in Dark Age, with more than one villager.** Source: Bunny, same thread.
- **Don't use the Town Bell.** Garrison only the villagers in danger, from their work site, then use return-to-work. Source: klnek and Bunny, same thread. Also ptee: "unbind it", https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=2993319677
- **Overplay warning.** Running from scouts, ringing the bell, and overbuilding spears against a few scouts are low-ELO mistakes. Fix: palisade resources and keep 1–2 spears near them. Source: FloosWorld, 2024-01-29, https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/forever-in-700-elo/248026
- **Spearmen in patrol near vulnerable villagers, plus walls. Reaching Castle first also works.** Source: https://ageofempires.substack.com/p/aoe2-scout-rush-guide
- **Don't chase scouts. Keep your own scouts near villagers.** Source: https://diamondlobby.com/age-of-empires-2/feudal-scout-rush-aoe2/
- **MAA vs scouts:** send at least one spear before clicking the MAA upgrade, and add a spear or two at home with small walls. Source: ptee guide (above).

### Archers
- **Skirmishers.** Elite skirmishers trade efficiently vs arbalests because they cost no gold. Losing some fights is fine while the opponent spends gold. Source: Nico, https://steamcommunity.com/app/813780/discussions/0/2914346777818133576
- **Don't overbuild spears vs archers.** Source: klnek, same thread.
- **Against large skirmisher counts, fall back behind walls and go up to Castle.** Source: ptee guide.
- **Scouts counter skirmishers.** Source: ptee guide; fandom Scout Cavalry "Strong vs Skirmishers…", https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Scout_Cavalry_(Age_of_Empires_II)

### Men-at-Arms
- **Archers counter MAA.** Source: https://diamondlobby.com/age-of-empires-2/man-at-arms-into-archers-aoe2/ ; Akfiz, https://steamcommunity.com/sharedfiles/filedetails/?id=3017973327 ; fandom MAA "Weak vs Archers, Scorpions", https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/Man-at-Arms_(Age_of_Empires_II)
- **Scouts vs MAA:** run scouts into the enemy base to pick off villagers, then follow up with one or two ranges. Source: ptee guide.
- **MAA die quickly when outnumbered**, including when mobbed by scout cavalry. Source: fandom MAA page.

### Drush
- **Fight with groups of 4–5 villagers and pull back hurt ones.** Hotkey villagers near the drush. Losing one villager won't decide a low-ELO game. Source: Cacomistle, 2021-01-20, https://steamcommunity.com/app/813780/discussions/0/3109140779861995493
- **Use walls and houses to funnel attackers to the TC.** Put a tower near gold or wood and aim for Feudal. Source: RedBack, ZeroFuqsGiven, same thread.
- **Early warning:** scout for a barracks before about 6:30 and check whether 10 gold is missing from the opponent's stockpile. Aztecs, Goths and Portuguese don't need it. Source: Fortniter and reply, same thread. Patch 185872 did not change these civs' Dark Age, per the notes.
- **Damage limitation, not a full counter.** The drush cost the opponent resources, so get your Feudal units out and you are about even. Source: 2005ChevyMalibu, same thread.
- **Scouts vs drush:** run your scouts straight at the enemy rather than clearing the drush at home. Leave one unit at home. Source: ptee guide.
- **Drush vs MAA:** don't fight with militia. Aim to cause idle time. Source: ptee guide.

### Towers
- **Cicero: towers are all-in and "not particularly recommended"** since Feudal towers lost HP. Source: https://johsaoe2debuildorderreference.site44.com/19-pop-towers.html
- Concrete defence steps were not read from a reliable AoE2 source. **UNVERIFIED.** A thread exists but was not opened: https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/how-to-beat-17-vill-teuton-tower-rush-in-arena/280386

### Fast Castle (opponent booming)
- **FC has no army for Feudal defence.** Source: https://guildorder.com/games/aoe2/wiki/build-orders-economic-timings
- Specific punish timings were not read. **UNVERIFIED.**

## (c) Unit counters (wiki tables + guides)

Fandom tables, read 2026-10-16. Pages: Skirmisher, Spearman, Knight, Archer, Man-at-Arms, Scout Cavalry, Camel Rider, Eagle Warrior, Mangonel, Monk, Cavalry Archer (Age of Empires II), each at `https://ageofempires.fandom.com/wiki/<Unit>_(Age_of_Empires_II)`. Eagle Warrior is at `/wiki/Eagle_Warrior`.

| Unit | Strong vs | Weak vs |
|---|---|---|
| Archer | Infantry, Villagers | Skirmishers, Scout Cavalry, Eagle Scouts |
| Skirmisher (60 res, no gold, 30 HP) | Archers, Spearmen | Melee units, siege |
| Spearman (60 res, no gold) | Cavalry | Archers, infantry |
| Man-at-Arms | Skirmishers, Scout Cav, Eagle Scouts, Spearmen, Villagers | Archers, Scorpions |
| Scout Cavalry | Skirmishers, Villagers, siege, Archers, Monks | Infantry, cavalry |
| Knight | Infantry, archers, Villagers, siege, CA, Light Cav | Pikemen, Camels, Monks, Elephants, Mamelukes, Teutonic Knights, Genoese Xbow, Boyars, Kamayuks, Berserks |
| Camel Rider | Skirmishers, siege, CA, cavalry | Infantry, archers, Cataphracts, defensive structures |
| Eagle Warrior | Archers, siege, Monks | Cataphracts, Boyars, Hand Cannoneers, Janissaries, Champions, Slingers |
| Cavalry Archer | Most infantry; foot archers with all armour techs | Long list: Elite Skirms, Mangonels, Scorpions, camels, elephants, etc. |
| Mangonel (295 total res) | Buildings, archers, siege, dense formations | Fast melee units (Huszars, Eagles), Mangudai, Monks with Redemption |
| Monk | Slow or expensive melee (Knights, elephants); Onagers with Redemption + Sanctity | Eagles, Light Cav, archers, siege, Heroes |

Practical rules:
- **"Use a gold unit as main damage dealer, paired with a non-gold unit that counters what beats it."** About 40 crossbowmen are needed to one-shot a knight in Castle. Source: Akfiz guide (above).
- **A valid counter must kill the unit, arrive fast enough, and not be undone by micro.** Mangonels or skirms can trap crossbows so knights can finish them. Source: ptee guide.
- **Official rules of thumb:** archer-line beats most infantry, spear-line beats most cavalry, cavalry beats most archers, militia-line is good vs non-gold units. Source: https://www.ageofempires.com/learn-to-play/military-economy-xbox/

### Patch 185872 counter changes (official/forum notes)
- **Mounted Crossbowman replaces CA for most European civs.** "Strong but slow attack". Cranequins gives +1 range and +2 vs infantry. Base stats are not published in the notes (**UNVERIFIED**). Franks' Ordonnance Companies (Castle, 400F 250G, 40s) cut its gold cost by 40%. Sources: official notes; forum patch thread.
- **Civs that gained MXB and lost CA:** Britons, Burgundians, Celts, Franks, Italians, Poles, Portuguese, Sicilians, Spanish, Teutons, Vikings, Bohemians (gain only). **Chinese lose the Heavy CA upgrade.** Source: forum patch thread.
- **Onager:** attack 50 → 55, pierce armour 7 → 8. Battering Ram LOS 3 → 5.
- **Huszar train time 12 → 18 s (Magyars).** Eagle Scout train time 50 → 46 s.
- **Anti-shock-infantry bonus:** MAA +3, Long Swordsman +8, 2H Swordsman +9, Champion +9. This counters the new Varangian Guard (shock infantry). Source: forum patch thread.
- **Jarl (Varangian UU) is "especially strong against infantry".** Varangian team bonus: Knight-line +damage vs infantry. Source: https://www.ageofempires.com/news/varangians-civilization-deep-dive/
- **Player opinion** (not data): MXB "fall off heavy in imp" and Cranequins is expensive (thebeerbandits, 2026-09-22). Skirmishers are still "insanely overused" (Pulikesi25, 2026-09-23). https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/balance-patch-22-09-2026-update-185872/289855?page=2 and ?page=3

## (d) Common mistakes at roughly 1000–1600 and their fixes
| Mistake | Fix | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Idle TC or villagers; playing too slowly | Review your recs and aim for zero idle time before Castle. "Stopping villager production for 30 seconds is the same as having a villager killed." Keep making villagers while attacked. | NastyHigh, 2024-01, https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/forever-in-700-elo/248026 |
| Stopping villagers at 50–60 | Keep the TC producing | FloosWorld, same thread |
| Floating resources (e.g. 2000 wood, no food or gold) | Spend, use the market, match eco to strategy. Build a farm every time you have about 60 spare wood. | WinFu1, same thread; ptee guide |
| Overreacting to small raids (bell, mass spears) | Garrison only threatened villagers, palisade resources, 1–2 spears | FloosWorld, same thread; ptee |
| Retreating from fights you win ("don't run from 5 pikes with 10 knights") | Judge army value | WinFu1, same thread |
| Skipping blacksmith or eco upgrades | Get upgrades for the unit you actually use. Lumber camp upgrades are top priority. | WinFu1; ptee; Akfiz (don't research everything) |
| Walking villagers to boar instead of luring | Lure boar to the TC | https://guildorder.com/games/aoe2/wiki/build-orders-economic-timings |
| No plan after the age-up; villagers left on depleted resources; too few farms | Pre-plan the transition and use idle-villager hotkeys | Same guildorder page; Akfiz (`.` / `Shift+.`) |
| Too much Dark Age walling that slows uptime | Hit timings first, then wall | ptee guide |
| Auto-scouting | Scout manually: resources, enemy buildings, army | ptee guide |
| Ignoring the minimap; idle production buildings | Check the minimap; calculate the villagers needed to keep production going | ptee guide |
| Mouse-only play, no build order | Learn one build and hotkeys vs easy AI | Player870583437, forever-in-700-elo thread |
| Fixing everything at once | Fix one problem per game | NastyHigh, same thread |
| Clumping archers; chasing into TC or castle fire | Patrol (Q-click) to spread fire; defensive stance (S); staggered formation (C) vs splash | Akfiz guide |

ELO-specific numbers (e.g. "1400 players click Feudal at X") were not available. AoE2 Coach compares against same-ELO winners but publishes no numbers (https://aoe2coach.net/). Cicero's A+..E grades (build_orders_current.md) are the only graded timing benchmarks found. They are not tied to ELO, so mapping grades to ELO is **UNVERIFIED**.

## (e) Civ tips: civs changed in 185872, plus the new civs

### New civs (Viking Sagas, released 2026-09-04)
Official bonuses: Danes (published 2026-08-28) https://www.ageofempires.com/news/danes-civilization-deep-dive/ ; Saxons (2026-08-31) https://www.ageofempires.com/news/saxons-civilization-deep-dive/ ; Varangians (2026-09-04) https://www.ageofempires.com/news/varangians-civilization-deep-dive/ . Exact numbers are not given in the deep dives (**UNVERIFIED** values). Stats are from SotL: https://sozai.app/transcript/strong-new-dlc-civs-aoe2-stats/index.md

- **Danes:**
  - *Bonuses:* fishing ships and villagers drop off extra food; Barracks and Siege Workshop upgrades cost less gold; loot part of each destroyed building's cost; Varangian Guard and Longships faster. Team bonus: siege +LOS.
  - *Units and techs:* UU Jomsviking (spear and shield, torches vs buildings and ships). UTs Hamask (infantry +damage as HP drops) and Northmen's Fury (siege +40% vs buildings, mangonel-line and siege ships +1 range).
  - *Stats:* about 47.5% win rate, best in 20–30 min games. Most common openings are FC/fish boom and MAA.
- **Saxons:**
  - *Bonuses:* dropsites give a little food and stone when built; foot soldiers cheaper per TC or Castle controlled; towers and castles fire more arrows from Castle Age; Longships and Catapult Galleons +HP. Team bonus: faster repairers.
  - *Units and techs:* UU Hearth Troop (melee plus periodic javelin). UTs Clerical Recruitment (monks +1 range, 33% faster training) and Shield Wall (infantry +armour in larger groups).
  - *Stats:* about 48%. Weak before 20 min, better late. Mostly FC.
- **Varangians:**
  - *Bonuses:* shepherding, fishing and hunting also generate gold; Bloodlines and Caravan effects increased; Varangian Guards attack faster and generate gold; Longships and Catapult Galleons attack faster. Team bonus: Knight-line +damage vs infantry.
  - *Units and techs:* UU Jarl (axe-throwing cavalry, strong vs infantry, "best used in combination with other soldiers"). UTs Vendel Legacy (knight-line trample) and Gothikon (Varangian Guards throw axes).
  - *Stats:* 45–47%, mostly scouts. SotL: "Gold is cheap in the early game, and food bonuses are king".
  - *Forum idea (not data):* archer opening plus market for early Bloodlines, then 130 HP knights. Source: SMUM15236, 2026-09-04, https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/new-dlc-the-viking-sagas/288281?page=48

### Existing civs changed in 185872
Source for all changes: https://forums.ageofempires.com/t/balance-patch-22-09-2026-update-185872/289855. Coaching implications are my inference, marked (inf.).

- **Magyars:** Huszar train time 12 → 18 s. (inf.) Huszar mass and replacement are slower.
- **Vikings:**
  - Chieftains no longer give gold when infantry kill villagers, trade carts or monks.
  - Lose Gambesons, CA and Elite Cannon Galleon. Gain Varangian Guard, MXB and Catapult Galleon.
  - (inf.) Drush/MAA gold snowball off villager kills is gone. Forum worries about Gambesons: NarrowBubbles54 and WhiteMagick4, 2026-09-23, page 2 of the patch thread.
- **Franks:**
  - Throwing Axeman range +2 (base 5, elite 6); Bearded Axe removed; Treadmill Crane added.
  - MXB replaces CA. Ordonnance Companies cuts MXB gold cost by 40%.
  - SotL: Franks scouts win 56% (aggregate).
- **Teutons:** the Castle/Imperial melee-armour bonus now covers infantry *and* mounted units. Elite Teutonic Knight melee armour 10 → 8. MXB replaces CA. Forum calls Teuton MXB "lackluster" (opinion).
- **Byzantines:**
  - Gain Elite Varangian Guard. Cataphract bonus vs infantry 9 → 13 (Elite 12 → 18).
  - Logistica now gives trample to Cataphracts and Varangian Guard instead of +6 vs infantry.
  - Forum: "now better" (opinion).
- **Burgundians:** Flemish Militia cost 30F → 35F, Feudal training 14 → 16 s; MXB replaces CA.
- **Britons, Celts, Poles, Spanish:** (Heavy) MXB replaces (Heavy) CA. Celts also lose Treadmill Crane.
- **Spanish:** Conquistador training 24 → 26 s, Elite cost 70 → 75F; Missionary now 30F 100G.
- **Italians, Portuguese, Sicilians:** MXB replaces CA. Sicilians also get Cranequins.
- **Portuguese:** also lose Redemption; Organ Gun training 25 → 28 s.
- **Vietnamese:**
  - Gain Masonry. The +20% HP bonus now applies only to foot archers and skirmishers.
  - Rattan Archer HP 40 → 35 (Elite 45 → 40); Elite Rattan upgrade 1000F → 800F.
  - (inf.) The CA and Fire Lancer HP bonus is gone (forum confirms CA nerf discussion).
- **Bengalis:** Ratha training 20 → 24 s; lose Treadmill Crane.
- **Bohemians:** Elite Hussite Wagon 80G; Wagenburg Tactics gunpowder speed 15% → 10%; gain MXB; lose Treadmill Crane.
- **Shu:** War Chariot 75F, training 32 s; gain Treadmill Crane.
- **Wei:** Xianbei Raider now 65W 35G.
- **Jurchens:** Grenadier 55F 50G.
- **Koreans:** lose Gambesons; Turtle Ship HP 260.
- **Mapuche:** Elite Bolas Rider 50W 55G.
- **Muisca:** Temple Guard attack 12 → 11.
- **Tupi:** Elite Blackwood Archer 40W.
- **Gurjaras:** lose the dock +5 garrison bonus.
- **Huns:** Atheism change.
- **Armenians and Goths:** gain Treadmill Crane. **Saracens:** lose it.
- **Forum summary (opinion):** FC unique-unit plays (Bolas, War Chariots, Fire Lancers) "heavily nerfed", making the meta "more concentrated" (Pulikesi25, 2026-09-23, page 3).
