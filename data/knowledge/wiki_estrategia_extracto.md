## 1. Patch 185872 global changes [OFFICIAL, P]
- New civs (Viking Sagas): Danes (Infantry + Siege), Saxons (Infantry + Defenses), Varangians (Cavalry + Navy). All ranked-legal.
- **Mounted Crossbowman** (regional, Archery Range) replaces Cavalry Archers for most European civs: "powerful but slow attack". **Cranequins**: MCB +1 range, +2 vs infantry. Numeric unit stats are not in the notes (UNVERIFIED).
- **Varangian Guard** (regional, Barracks): shock infantry that generates gold when fighting units; affected by Gambesons. Civs: Byzantines, Danes, Saxons, Varangians, Vikings.
- Longboat renamed **Longship** (Vikings, Danes, Saxons, Varangians).
- Anti-shock-infantry bonuses: Man-at-Arms +2 → +3; Long Swordsman +6 → +8; Two-Handed Swordsman/Champion +8 → +9.
- Eagle Scout train time 50s → 46s.
- Onager attack 50 → 55, pierce armor 7 → 8.
- LoS: Ram line 3 → 5; Armored Elephant line 4 → 6; Siege Tower 8 → 6.
- Demolition Ship upgrade 50w/100g/50s → 25w/75g/20s.
- Hulk melee armor 4/5/6 → 3/4/5; Hulk HP 90 → 85; War Hulk HP 115 → 110.
- Lou Chuan vs buildings +230 → +180; Fire Galley vs buildings 1 → 0.
- Fortified Church build time 40 → 50s.
- Maps: new **Arabian Desert** (open, scarce resources, King of the Desert style). Black Forest ponds +50%. Land Nomad and Ring Fortress resource/relic distribution reworked. Thames forests/crossings revised.

## 3. New civs: bonuses with numbers [STATS, aoe2insights; deep dives give text only]
Resource labels for costs are missing on aoe2insights; the f/g order is assumed and UNVERIFIED.
- **Danes** (https://www.aoe2insights.com/civilization/danes/): Fishing Ships and Villagers +5% food on drop-off; loot 25% of the cost of each destroyed building; Barracks and Siege Workshop upgrades −66% gold; Varangian Guard and Longship +10% speed. Team: siege +2 LoS. UU Jomsviking (costs "65/15", 10s; throws torches at buildings/ships). UTs: Northmen's Fury (950/900, 40s: siege and siege ships +40% vs buildings, Mangonel line and Catapult Galleon +1 range); Hamask (250/450, 35s: infantry deal more damage as they lose HP). Win rate 50.5% (n=747). Feudal ~10:08, Castle ~20:21. Unit mix 57.5% infantry.
- **Saxons** (https://www.aoe2insights.com/civilization/saxons/): Mill, Lumber Camp and Mining Camp give +35 food +10 stone when built; foot soldiers −5% cost per TC or Castle controlled (max −20%); Towers and Castles +100% base arrows from Castle Age; Longship and Catapult Galleon +20% HP. Team: repairers +25%. UU Hearth Troop ("80/35", 16s; Elite 14s; javelin then melee). UTs: Shield Wall (525/475, 45s: infantry armor when massed); Clerical Recruitment (350/200, 35s: Monks +1 range, train 33% faster). Win rate 54.2% (n=878, rank #1/57; small sample).
- **Varangians** (https://www.aoe2insights.com/civilization/varangians/): shepherding, fishing and hunting also generate gold (amount not given); Bloodlines and Caravan effects +50%; Varangian Guard attacks 25% faster with +50% gold generation; Longship and Catapult Galleon attack 15% faster. Team: Knight line +1 attack vs infantry. UU Jarl ("75/55", 23s; axe-throwing cavalry, anti-infantry). UTs: Gothikon (475/400, 50s: Varangian Guards throw axes); Vendel Legacy (500/300, 35s: Knight line trample). Win rate 46.7% (n=1,301, #56/57). Unit mix: 47% cavalry (Scouts, Knights), 14% Varangian Guard, 14% skirmishers.
- Early matchup hints (very small samples, UNVERIFIED): Saxons vs Varangians ~61.8% for Saxons; Varangians vs Danes ~55.6% for Varangians.

## 4. Established civs [STATS, aoe2insights RM 1v1]
- **Franks** (/civilization/franks/): 50.6% (n=237,716, #17/57); pick 6.8% at <850 Elo down to 1.8% at ≥1900. Bonuses: foragers +15%; Mill techs free; mounted +20% HP from Feudal; Castles −15%/−25% in Castle/Imperial. Team: Knight line +2 LoS. UTs: Chivalry (Stables +40% work rate), Ordonnance Companies. Best vs Muisca 59.2%, Britons 57.0%, Dravidians 56.2%, Khmer 55.4%. Worst vs Khitans 42.4%, Romans 45.3%, Armenians 46.8%, Teutons 46.9%. Typical Feudal ~10:17, Castle ~21:04.
- **Britons** (/civilization/britons/): 47.5% (n=160,689, **#55/57**; likely hit by losing CA in 185872, UNVERIFIED cause). Shepherds +25%; TCs −50% wood from Castle Age; foot archers +1/+2 range in Castle/Imperial. Team: Archery Ranges +10% speed. Longbowman ("35/40", 18s). Yeomen (750/450, 60s: foot archers and skirmishers +1 range, towers +2 attack); Warwolf (800/400, 40s: Trebuchet blast damage). Best vs Saracens 55.5%, Italians 54.5%, Ethiopians 53.7%. Worst vs Wu 40.3%, Shu 40.4%, Hindustanis 42.0%, Khmer 42.5%, Franks 43.0%, Huns 43.1%.
- **Mongols** (/civilization/mongols/): 51.1% (n=414,806, #14); pick ~6.1% (most played). Hunters +40%; CA attack +25% faster; Scout line and Steppe Lancer +20/30% HP in Castle/Imperial. Team: Scout line +2 LoS. Mangudai ("55/65", 26s). Drill (Siege Workshop units +50% speed; 500/450, 60s); Nomads (lost houses keep pop; 300/150, 40s). Feudal ~9:37 (fastest seen). Best vs Varangians 56.3%, Jurchens 56.0%, Burmese 55.8%, Poles 55.7%, Teutons 55.7%. Worst vs Saxons 44.4%, Gurjaras 46.2%, Hindustanis 46.2%, Mapuche 46.3%, Huns 46.5%, Shu 47.2%.
- **Khitans** (/civilization/khitans/): 53.8% (n=79,492, **#3/57**). Pastures replace Farms; melee attack upgrades doubled; Skirmisher, Spearman and Scout lines train and upgrade 15% faster; Heavy CA available in Castle Age at −50% cost. Team: infantry +2 vs ranged soldiers. Liao Dao ("40/40", 12s). Ordo Cavalry (600/300: cavalry regen in combat); Lamellar Armor (450/300: infantry and skirmishers reflect 25% melee damage). Best vs Georgians 63.1%, Lithuanians 61.9%, Poles 61.3%, Slavs 60.4%, Khmer 59.9%. Worst vs Mapuche 43.0%, Muisca 45.3%, Romans 48.5%. Opening is mostly scouts (29.6% of units).
- aoestats default view (patch **162286, outdated**), top win rates: Romans 54.14%, Bulgarians 53.41%, Vikings 52.46%, Malay 52.16%, Wu 52.12% (https://aoestats.io/). Do NOT use as current.
