# AoE2 DE — Civilizations, maps, ranked pools (researched 2026-10-16, patch 185872)

## Civilizations

Source: the civ navigation menu at https://www.aoe2insights.com/civilization/franks/, which lists **59** entries. Achaemenids, Athenians and Spartans are **Chronicles: Battle for Greece** civs. They are playable only in the Chronicles mode, not ranked RM.

That gives **56 standard (ranked-playable) civilizations**. The count is derived and cross-checked against the DLC history:
- Base DE: 35 (official game page: "35 different civilizations", https://www.ageofempires.com/games/aoeiide/).
- Plus Lords of the West, Dawn of the Dukes, Dynasties of India, Return of Rome, The Mountain Royals, The Three Kingdoms.
- Plus The Last Chieftains: 3.
- Plus The Viking Sagas: 3.

The same aoe2insights page shows a tier rank "#18 / 57", which does not match 56. The exact ranked count of 56 is therefore *derived, not stated* by any source.
