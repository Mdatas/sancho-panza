## Update 185872 — 22 Sep 2026 (CURRENT)

### New content (185872)
- **New civs:**
  - **Danes:** Infantry & Siege.
  - **Saxons:** Infantry & Defense.
  - **Varangians:** Cavalry & Navy.
- **New regional units and techs:**
  - **Mounted Crossbowman:** replaces Cavalry Archer for 12 European civs: Bohemians, Britons, Burgundians, Celts, Franks, Italians, Poles, Portuguese, Sicilians, Spanish, Teutons, Vikings. The Viking Sagas civs get it too.
  - **Cranequins (Imperial):** Mounted Crossbowmen get +1 range and +2 attack vs Infantry. Britons, Franks and Sicilians gain access. No unit stats were given in the notes (UNVERIFIED).
  - **Varangian Guard (Barracks, Castle Age):** shock infantry that generates gold in combat and is affected by Gambesons. Available to Vikings, Byzantines and the Viking Sagas civs.
  - **Longship:** the Longboat is renamed and is now available to Vikings, Danes, Saxons and Varangians.
- **New map:** Arabian Desert, "a more open competitive version of Arabia".
- **Other features:**
  - New terrains, trees and animals (Spruce, Gorse, Heather, Lynx, Elk, Seal, Pheasant, Raven).
  - AI and pathfinding rework; Stand Ground Patrol now limits overlapping melee attacks.
  - Ranked Deathmatch lobby: Team Positions and Turbo Mode changed from Locked to Unlocked.
  - The notes list **no ranked map pool change**.

### New civ kits (from official deep dives) (185872)
- **Danes** (https://www.ageofempires.com/news/danes-civilization-deep-dive/, published 2026-08-28)
  - Civ bonuses:
    - Fishing Ships and Villagers drop off a small amount of extra food.
    - Barracks and Siege Workshop upgrades cost less gold.
    - They loot part of the resource cost of each destroyed building.
    - Varangian Guards and Longships move faster.
  - Team bonus: siege weapons get more line of sight.
  - Unique unit: **Jomsviking**, anti-cavalry spear-and-shield infantry that throws torches at buildings and ships.
  - Unique techs:
    - **Hamask:** infantry deal more damage as they lose HP.
    - **Northmen's Fury:** siege units and siege ships get +40% attack vs buildings; the Mangonel line and siege ships get +1 range.
  - Exact numbers: UNVERIFIED (the deep dive has no numbers).
- **Saxons** (https://www.ageofempires.com/news/saxons-civilization-deep-dive/, published 2026-08-31)
  - Civ bonuses:
    - Dropsites give a little extra food and stone when built.
    - Foot soldiers cost less for each Town Center or Castle controlled.
    - Towers and Castles fire more arrows from the Castle Age.
    - Longships and Catapult Galleons have more HP.
  - Team bonus: repairers work faster.
  - Unique unit: **Hearth Troop**, heavy, slow, high-armor sword infantry that periodically throws a javelin.
  - Unique techs:
    - **Clerical Recruitment:** Monks get +1 range and train 33% faster.
    - **Shield Wall:** infantry gain more armor in larger groups.
  - Exact numbers: UNVERIFIED.
- **Varangians** (https://www.ageofempires.com/news/varangians-civilization-deep-dive/, published 2026-09-04)
  - Civ bonuses:
    - Shepherding, fishing and hunting also generate gold.
    - Bloodlines and Caravan effects are increased.
    - Varangian Guards attack faster and generate gold.
    - Longships and Catapult Galleons attack faster.
  - Team bonus: the Knight line deals more damage vs infantry.
  - Unique unit: **Jarl**, axe-throwing cavalry that is strong vs infantry.
  - Unique techs:
    - **Vendel Legacy:** the Knight line deals trample damage.
    - **Gothikon:** Varangian Guards periodically throw axes.
  - Exact numbers: UNVERIFIED.
- The deep dives do not say which unique tech is the Castle Age tech and which is the Imperial Age tech. **UNVERIFIED.**
